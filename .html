Shark Facts!

Sharks have no bones.
There skeleton is made of bendy cartilage, just like your ears or nose!

Sharks lose lots of teeth.
Sharks can grow over 20,000 teeth in a lifetime!

Baby sharks are called pups.
A mother shark can have anywhere from 2 to over 100 pups at once!

Sharks where here before dinosaurs.
They have been swimming in the ocean for over 450 million years!

A group of sharks are called a 'shiver'.
They can also be called a school; or a frenzy when there feeding!
